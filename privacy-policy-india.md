# Privacy Policy for Ollvy

**Last Updated:** January 15, 2026

**Effective Date:** January 15, 2026

---

At Ollvy, your privacy is our top priority. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use the Ollvy mobile application ("App"). This policy is drafted in accordance with the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA)**, the **Information Technology Act, 2000**, and the **Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011** of India.

By downloading, installing, or using Ollvy, you consent to the collection, processing, and storage of your data as described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Definitions

For the purposes of this Privacy Policy:

- **"Data Principal"** means the individual to whom the personal data relates (i.e., you, the user)
- **"Data Fiduciary"** means Ollvy, which determines the purpose and means of processing personal data
- **"Personal Data"** means any data about an individual who is identifiable by or in relation to such data
- **"Sensitive Personal Data"** includes biometric data, health data, and other categories as defined under applicable Indian law
- **"Processing"** includes collection, storage, use, and erasure of personal data

---

## 2. Data Collection

### 2.1 Data We Collect

Ollvy collects the following types of data to provide you with personalized skin analysis:

| Data Type | Purpose | Storage Location |
|-----------|---------|------------------|
| **3D Facial Scans** | Skin analysis using TrueDepth camera | Device only (local) |
| **Skin Analysis Results** | Generate metrics about skin texture and characteristics | Device only (local) |
| **Photos/Images** | Store scan results for progress tracking | Device only (local) |
| **Device Information** | Crash reporting and app stability | Transmitted to Sentry (anonymized) |

### 2.2 Data We Do NOT Collect

- Name, email, phone number, or any personal identification
- Aadhaar number or government ID
- Location data or GPS coordinates
- Contacts or address book
- Financial information (PAN, bank details, UPI)
- Data from other apps on your device

### 2.3 Sensitive Personal Data

Under Indian law, biometric data (including facial geometry) is classified as Sensitive Personal Data. Ollvy processes this data with the following safeguards:

- **100% on-device processing** - No facial data is transmitted to any server
- **No cloud storage** - All biometric data remains on your device
- **User control** - You can delete all data at any time
- **Explicit consent** - Camera permission is requested before any scanning

---

## 3. Data Storage and Security

### 3.1 Local-Only Processing

Your privacy is fundamental to how Ollvy works:

- **100% Local Processing:** All facial scans and skin analysis are processed entirely on your device. No facial data ever leaves your iPhone.
- **No Cloud Upload:** We do not upload your face data, scans, or analysis results to any cloud servers or remote systems.
- **No Server Transmission:** Your facial data and scan results are never transmitted over the internet.
- **Device-Only Storage:** All data is stored securely in your device's local storage, protected by iOS security features including encryption at rest.
- **No Account Required:** Ollvy works completely offline and does not require you to create an account or sign in.

### 3.2 Security Measures

In compliance with the IT (SPDI) Rules, 2011, we implement the following security practices:

- **Encryption:** All data stored on your device is protected by iOS's built-in encryption
- **Access Control:** Only you can access your scan data through your device
- **No Network Exposure:** Since facial data never leaves your device, it cannot be intercepted
- **Secure Development:** The app is developed following industry-standard secure coding practices

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

### 4.1 Sentry (Crash Reporting)

Ollvy uses **Sentry** for crash reporting and error monitoring to improve app stability.

| What is Sent | What is NOT Sent |
|--------------|------------------|
| Anonymized crash logs | Facial data or scans |
| Device model (e.g., iPhone 14) | Photos or images |
| iOS version | Personal information |
| Error stack traces | Location data |
| Anonymous session ID | Name, email, or contacts |

- **Sentry Privacy Policy:** https://sentry.io/privacy/
- **Data Location:** Sentry servers are located in the United States. Only anonymized technical data is transmitted.

### 4.2 Services We Do NOT Use

- Advertising networks or ad tracking
- Analytics services that track user behavior
- Social media integrations
- Any services that share, sell, or monetize your data

## 5. Data Principal Rights (Under DPDPA 2023)

As a Data Principal under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the following rights:

### 5.1 Right to Access
You can view all your saved scans and analysis results within the app at any time.

### 5.2 Right to Correction
Since all data is stored locally and you have full control, you can manage your data directly within the app.

### 5.3 Right to Erasure
You can:
- Delete individual scans by swiping left in your history
- Delete all data from Settings > Privacy > Delete All Data
- Uninstall the app to permanently remove all associated data

### 5.4 Right to Data Portability
You can export your scan results as PDF documents or JSON files at any time from within the app.

### 5.5 Right to Withdraw Consent
You can withdraw consent at any time by:
- Revoking camera permissions in iOS Settings
- Deleting your data within the app
- Uninstalling the application

### 5.6 Right to Grievance Redressal
You have the right to file a complaint with our Grievance Officer or the Data Protection Board of India.

## 6. Consent

### 6.1 How We Obtain Consent

By using Ollvy, you provide consent in the following ways:

- **Camera Permission:** iOS prompts you to grant camera access before any scanning. You can revoke this permission at any time in iOS Settings.
- **App Usage:** Your continued use of the app after reviewing this Privacy Policy constitutes consent to our data practices.
- **Explicit Actions:** Initiating a scan is an explicit action that indicates consent to process facial data for that scan.

### 6.2 Withdrawal of Consent

You may withdraw your consent at any time by:
- Disabling camera permissions for Ollvy in iOS Settings
- Deleting your scan data within the app
- Uninstalling the application

Withdrawal of consent will not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.

|-----------|------------------|-----------------|
