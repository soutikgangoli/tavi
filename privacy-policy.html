Privacy Policy for Ollvy

Last Updated: December 24, 2025

At Ollvy, your privacy is our top priority. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use the Ollvy app. We believe in complete transparency about what data we collect, how we use it, and where it's stored.

1. Data Collection

Ollvy collects the following types of data to provide you with personalized skin analysis:

  • 3D Facial Scans: We use your device's TrueDepth camera to capture 3D facial scans for skin analysis purposes.
  • Skin Analysis Results: The app generates and stores analysis results based on your facial scans, including metrics about skin health, texture, and other characteristics.
  • Photos: When you choose to save your scans, they are stored locally on your device as photos in the app's private storage.
  • Device Information: We collect basic device information (device model, iOS version) only for crash reporting purposes to help us improve app stability.

We do NOT collect:

  • Your name, email, or any personal identification
  • Location data
  • Contacts or address book
  • Any data from other apps on your device

2. Data Storage

Your privacy is fundamental to how Ollvy works:

  • 100% Local Processing: All facial scans and skin analysis are processed entirely on your device. No facial data ever leaves your iPhone.
  • No Cloud Upload: We do not upload your face data, scans, or analysis results to any cloud servers or remote systems.
  • No Server Transmission: Your facial data and scan results are never transmitted over the internet.
  • Device-Only Storage: All data is stored securely in your device's local storage, protected by iOS security features.
  • No Account Required: Ollvy works completely offline and does not require you to create an account or sign in.

3. Third-Party Services

Ollvy uses minimal third-party services to ensure app stability:

Sentry (Crash Reporting)

We use Sentry for crash reporting and error monitoring. This helps us identify and fix bugs to improve the app experience.

  • Data Sent to Sentry: Anonymized crash logs, device model, iOS version, and error stack traces
  • Purpose: App stability, bug fixes, and performance improvements
  • What is NOT Sent: No facial data, scan results, photos, or any personal information is sent to Sentry
  • Sentry Privacy Policy: https://sentry.io/privacy/

We do NOT use:

  • Advertising networks
  • Analytics services that track user behavior
  • Social media integrations
  • Any services that share or sell your data

4. Data Retention

You have complete control over your data:

  • Local Storage Only: All data remains on your device until you choose to delete the app or remove individual scans.
  • Export Capability: You can export your scan results as PDF documents at any time.
  • Delete Individual Scans: You can delete any individual scan from the app at any time.
  • Complete Deletion: Deleting the Ollvy app from your device permanently removes all associated data.

5. User Rights

As an Ollvy user, you have the following rights:

  • Access to Your Data: View all your saved scans and analysis results within the app at any time.
  • Delete Your Data: Remove individual scans or delete the entire app to erase all data.
  • Export Your Data: Generate and share PDF reports of your skin analysis results.
  • No Account Lock-In: Since Ollvy doesn't require an account, your data is always under your control on your device.

6. Contact Information

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us:

  • Email: support@ollvy.app
  • App Store Support: Visit the Ollvy app page on the App Store and use the "App Support" link

7. Children's Privacy

Ollvy is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, regulatory, or operational reasons. When we make changes:

  • The "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy will be revised
  • Significant changes will be communicated through app updates or in-app notifications
  • Your continued use of Ollvy after changes indicates acceptance of the updated policy

9. Data Security

We take data security seriously:

  • iOS Security: All data stored on your device is protected by iOS's built-in security features, including encryption at rest.
  • No Network Transmission: Since facial data never leaves your device, it cannot be intercepted during transmission.
  • Secure Crash Reporting: Crash reports sent to Sentry are transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.

10. Legal Basis for Processing

Ollvy processes your data based on:

  • Your Consent: By using the app, you consent to the collection and processing of data as described in this policy.
  • Legitimate Interest: We have a legitimate interest in improving app stability and fixing bugs through crash reporting.

11. International Users

Ollvy is designed to work entirely on your device, regardless of your location. Since we don't transmit facial data to servers, there are no international data transfers of your scan results. Crash reports sent to Sentry may be processed in the United States, where Sentry's servers are located.

12. Your California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, you have specific rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to know what personal information is collected
  • Right to delete personal information
  • Right to opt-out of the sale of personal information (Note: Ollvy does not sell personal information)

Disclaimer

Important: Ollvy is a skin analysis tool for general awareness and skincare insights only. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. The app does not provide medical advice. For any skin health concerns, please consult a qualified dermatologist or healthcare professional.

This privacy policy is effective as of December 24, 2025, and applies to all users of the Ollvy app.
