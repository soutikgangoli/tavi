App Review Notes:
---

DEVICE REQUIREMENT: This app requires iPhone X or newer with TrueDepth camera (Face ID hardware). Testing on simulator or incompatible devices will show a graceful error message.

HOW TO TEST THE APP:

1. Launch app - onboarding will explain TrueDepth requirement
2. Grant camera permission when prompted (permission text explains local processing)
3. Tap "Start Scan" from home screen
4. Position face in frame - app will guide alignment
5. Hold steady for 10 seconds while scan completes
6. View results screen with skin metrics and visualizations
7. IMPORTANT: Scroll to bottom of results to see medical disclaimer
8. Tap individual metrics to see detailed analysis
9. Optional: Export as PDF or save scan to compare later

PRIVACY & DATA:
- All face data processed locally on-device (no server transmission)
- Crash reports sent to Sentry with anonymized diagnostic data only
- No facial data, photos, or personal information transmitted to any server
- Users can delete all scans at any time from Settings

MEDICAL DISCLAIMER:
- Clear disclaimer shown on ALL results screens
- App provides general skincare insights only (not medical advice)
- Users directed to consult dermatologist for medical concerns
- No prescription drug recommendations
- No diagnostic claims

TESTING NOTES:
- Best tested on iPhone 12 or newer for optimal TrueDepth performance
- Good lighting required for accurate scans
- Scan quality message will appear if lighting is insufficient
- App gracefully handles all error states (camera denied, device unsupported, etc.)
