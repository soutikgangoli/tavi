Ollvy: AI-Powered Skin Analysis for Indian Skin

Discover personalized skin insights built for Indian skin tones. Ollvy uses advanced 3D facial scanning and AI to help you understand your skin health—completely privately on your device.

WHY OLLVY?

Built for Indian Skin
Unlike generic beauty apps, Ollvy is optimized for Indian skin tones (Fitzpatrick Types III-VI). Our AI recognizes the unique characteristics of melanin-rich skin, including higher melanin content, distinct aging patterns, and specific pigmentation concerns that most beauty tech overlooks.

100% Privacy-First
Your face data never leaves your device. Every scan is processed locally on your iPhone. No account required. No cloud uploads. No servers. You own your data.
